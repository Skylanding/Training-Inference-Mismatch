# DLC under continuous asynchrony

Paper, App. C (`tab:async`). Code: `tim/dlc/async_hooks.py` (verl glue), `tim/dlc/async_versions.py` (pure parts,
tested in `tests/test_async_versions.py`). Launcher: `SOURCE=async` in `scripts/train.sh`.

## Configuration

verl's v1 separate-async trainer with partial rollout: one trainer GPU and one standalone sampler GPU
(`hybrid_engine=False`, `N_GPUS=2`). The trainer pushes its weights to the sampler over NCCL after every training step
(`parameter_sync_step=1`); a push aborts the requests in flight, loads the weights and resumes them with the prompt
and the tokens generated so far. The maximum version gap is four (`max_off_policy_threshold=4`, strategy `wait`),
three batches are generated ahead (`num_warmup_batches=3`), and the sampler runs at most 64 sequences at a time, so
that the generation of a batch outlasts a training step and responses span weight pushes. Everything else is the
configuration of the other Qwen3-1.7B trainings (Countdown, 40 steps, a checkpoint every five steps).

## Per-token versions

- **Sampler server.** The version of a generation segment is the server's `global_steps` when the request is admitted
  to the engine. The admission gate closes before every weight push and reopens after the new version is set, so every
  token of a segment comes from these weights, also when the segment is aborted.
- **Client.** The partial-rollout resume loop collects (version, number of tokens) per segment and expands them to one
  version per generated token.
- **Output.** The versions travel as the TransferQueue field `rollout_token_versions` (response-level, int64).

## Trainer side (`TIM_ASYNC_OLD=version`)

`PPOTrainerSeparateAsync._compute_old_log_prob` is replaced. The current weights (version cur = global step − 1) are
saved to CPU, and the newest `ASYNC_KEEP` = 5 versions are kept. verl's own forward gives log p_θ_t; for every other
version in the batch the trainer restores that version from CPU, runs a forward and keeps the tokens generated by it;
then the current weights are restored. The result is `old_log_probs` = log p_θ_k(t) per token, and, for DLC, the
sampler's top-128 head log-probs at the generating version, merged per token into `ref_topk_log_probs`, which the
centering term of `tim/dlc/hooks.py` uses as p_old. Tokens of a version without a snapshot mask their response.

## Methods

| Paper name | `METHOD` | Ratio and correction |
|---|---|---|
| policy gradient | `pg` | bypass mode, no ratio; version statistics only |
| importance sampling | `is` | token ratio p_θ / q capped at 2 (denominator: the sampler's log-probs) |
| M2PO | `m2po` | M2PO mask on p_θ / p_θ_k(t) |
| DLC | `dlc_async` | clip 0.2 on ρ_t = p_θ / p_θ_k(t), centering term with p_old = p_θ_k(t) |
| DLC with admission | `dlc_admission` | `dlc_async` plus ESS admission |

**ESS admission.** For each response, ESS = 1 / mean_t(ρ_t²) over its tokens. A response with ESS below
`ESS_MIN` = 0.5 is masked, and its prompt is submitted again under a fresh id (the whole group of eight samples, since
verl generates a prompt's samples together), at most three times per original prompt. In GRPO the group mean still
includes the reward of the masked response, its own advantage is zeroed by the mask, and the token-mean denominator
drops its tokens.

## Measurements logged per step

`tim/async_tok_gap_mean`, `tim/async_tok_gap_max` and `tim/async_tok_gap_frac_g{0..7,8plus}` (version gap of the
sampled tokens), `tim/async_resp_multi_version_frac` (responses generated across two or more versions),
`tim/async_old_minus_q_abs_mean_{all,fresh,stale}` (|log p_θ_k(t) − log q|: bf16 noise for every gap),
`tim/async_ess_{mean,min,deferred,defer_frac,deferred_total}` (admission), and the lag metrics `tim/split_lag_*` and
`tim/split_chi2_token` against the generating versions.
